# BiryaniPepsi

### *“Every biryani has a region, a flavour, a fanbase, and a story.”*

### *Crafted for true biryani enthusiasts.*

BiryaniPepsi is a modern and interactive biryani encyclopedia platform built to explore the culture, flavour, uniqueness, history, and regional identity of biryani across India and South Asia. The project focuses on discovery and experience rather than recipes, treating every biryani like a unique personality with its own story, spice profile, loyal fanbase, and local pride.

The platform combines the feel of a food atlas, a cultural archive, and a community-driven food discovery website. Instead of simply listing dishes, the website explains why certain biryanis became iconic, how they differ from one another, what type of people may enjoy them, and which places are most popularly associated with them.

---

# Project Overview

BiryaniPepsi is designed around the idea that biryani is more than just food. Different regions have developed completely different styles of biryani over time, influenced by local spices, rice varieties, royal kitchens, coastal traditions, military hotels, and street-food culture.

The website documents major biryani identities including Hyderabadi, Lucknowi, Kolkata, Ambur, Dindigul, Donne, Malabar, Bhatkali, Memoni, Kalyani, Kampuri, Cuttacki, and many more, while also covering ingredient-based and modern variants such as Egg Biryani, Paneer Biryani, Chicken 65 Biryani, Fish Biryani, and Bamboo Biryani.

The platform is built to function as a searchable and visual encyclopedia where users can:
- explore biryanis by state and region
- compare flavour and spice profiles
- discover famous local places
- understand cultural background and uniqueness
- browse through public recommendations and food experiences
- contribute suggestions and reviews for future updates

# Project Vision

BiryaniPepsi is built as a digital archive of biryani culture. The goal is to make biryani exploration fun, visual, informative, and community-driven while preserving the uniqueness and regional identity of different biryani styles.

The platform focuses on:
- cultural storytelling
- flavour discovery
- regional food mapping
- public food experiences
- and community-driven recommendations

Rather than functioning like a recipe website, the project aims to become a searchable encyclopedia where users can understand the identity and personality of biryani from different regions.
